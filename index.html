    Sources and how sure we are

    Every fact on this tree carries a badge. Proven means an obituary or record states it directly. Probable means records agree but haven't been checked against an original document. Possible means a lead from another researcher's online tree that still needs proof. Family memory is what someone in the family remembers — often the most valuable part, and worth checking too.

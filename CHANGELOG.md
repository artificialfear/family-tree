# Changelog

## 2026-11-23 — Roots lower
- The roots and their hometowns now spread out below the grandchildren instead of running into them. The tree stays the same size; drag up a little to see the lower towns.

## 2026-11-23 — Story in order
- "Johnnie and Annie Mae" now comes after "Jack and Margaret's eight" (1956) and stays undated, since their marriage year isn't known. The story now runs in date order: the Graham family, then the Harper family, then 1982, when Ed and Renee bring the two together.

## 2026-11-23 — Full-screen button
- A small round button in the top-left corner switches the browser to full screen and back. Phones and tablets no longer go full screen by themselves, so this lets anyone choose it. It doesn't appear where the browser can't do full screen (iPhone Safari) or when the tree was opened from a home-screen icon, which is already full screen.

## 2026-11-23 — Steady tree while the sky changes
- Switching between night and sunset no longer touches the tree. The fireflies now fade out together instead of being switched off one by one, and the faint low stars stay put. Before, the tablet had to redraw the layer over the tree, and photos and name scrolls flickered for a moment.
- Phones and tablets keep a smaller picture of the tree in memory, which leaves the tablet more room during the sky change.

## 2026-11-23 — Tablets, round 2
- Phones and tablets no longer switch the browser to full screen when the tree opens. That switch is what turned an Android tablet's screen black for about 3 seconds, hiding the curtains. To get full screen without the black screen, add the site to the home screen (Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen). It then opens full screen like an app, with its own crescent-moon icon.
- Smoother sky change: the moon, sun, stars and clouds now wait just above the stage instead of far off-screen, so the tablet keeps them drawn. This fixes the grey square where the moon or sun came in, and the half-drawn sky during the change.
- Desktop computers still go full screen as before.

## 2026-11-23 — Map in the story
- Each story chapter shows a small map in the corner of the card, with that chapter's town glowing gold and earlier towns as faint dots. Georgia chapters zoom in on Georgia.

## 2026-11-23 — Tablets
- Held upright, a tablet now hangs the gold stars and the sunset clouds in the open sky between the banner and the top of the tree, not bunched in the corners or tangled in the branches. Landscape, phones and computers look the same as before.
- Smoother night ⇄ sunset change: the sky no longer repaints itself on every frame, which showed up as black or blocky patches on Android tablets.
- Tablets use the lighter graphics that phones already use: a smaller canopy picture, and the tree fades to its new view instead of sliding. On touch screens the curtains also wait a moment longer for full screen to settle before they part.

## 2026-11-23 — "For Mom" in Mat's words; Johnnie
- The last chapter, "For Mom", now ends with Mat's own words, including Annie Mae as the glue that held the family together and "We love you very much, momma!"
- Johnnie Harper's name is spelled "Johnnie" everywhere (the spelling on his 1940 census record).
- The 1956 chapter now says what happened that year: Jack and Margaret's first child, Richard, was born.

## 2026-11-23 — Story music on cue, paced auto-play
- "Begin the story" now starts the story song on its first note, at full volume, right as the curtains open (the same as "Explore").
- Auto-play gives each chapter as long as it needs to read (about 9 to 18 seconds, longer chapters stay up longer) instead of a flat 11 seconds. A thin gold line under the buttons fills while each chapter is showing.

## 2026-11-22 — Add a memory from the story
- The last story chapter has an "Add a memory" button. It asks who the memory is about (Ed, Renee, Johnny, Annie Mae, Jack, Margaret, or someone else), then closes the story and opens that person's memory form. "Someone else" returns to the tree with a tip to tap anyone's face.
- The "Tap any face" tip no longer shows on top of the story card.

## 2026-11-22 — Cleaner sky
- Removed the newest-memory line under the title banner. Family memories still appear as gold stars beside each person on the tree.

## 2026-11-22 — Music on cue
- Choosing "Explore" now starts the tree song on its first piano chord, at full volume, right as the curtains open.

## 2026-11-22 — New music, and story music
- The tree has a new background song, and the story now has its own song that starts when the story begins and blends back into the tree song when the story ends.
- Both songs start right away (no silence at the start) and loop smoothly without their endings.

## 2026-11-22 — Easier to read on phones
- On phones the tree opens larger, filling the space between the sky and the buttons, and the names on the scrolls are bigger. The outer cousins are a swipe away at the sides.
- The story card on phones is slimmer, with smaller photos and text; longer chapters scroll inside the card.
- Finishing the story with "Explore the tree" now changes the sky: night turns to sunset (or sunset to night).
- Behind the scenes: removed old designs that are no longer used (earlier tree styles, the growing-tree opening, faces on the sun and moon, the oval title plaque, red curtains, and the in-tree banner and constellations).

## 2026-11-22 — Lowered in on opening
- When the curtains first open, the moon and the hanging stars are lowered down into place on their strings.

## 2026-11-22 — Stars out, clouds in
- When the sky turns to sunset, the hanging gold stars are hoisted up one after another behind the curtain, and peach paper clouds are lowered in on strings beside the tree. Going back to night, the clouds go up and the stars come back down.

## 2026-11-22 — Brighter, steady sunset
- The sunset sky is brighter and warmer, and it no longer gets darker or lighter as you move around the tree.

## 2026-11-22 — Night and sunset scene change
- The sky button now simply switches between night and sunset (the automatic mode is gone).
- Switching is a little scene change: the paper moon is pulled up behind the curtain on its string, then the paper sun is lowered down on its strings while the night sky slowly fades into sunset — and the reverse going back to night.

## 2026-11-22 — Paper-cut sun
- At sunset the sun is now a gold paper-cut sun with pointed rays, hanging on two strings from behind the curtain — a matching prop to the paper moon. Tap it for the note for Mom.

## 2026-11-22 — Velvet pennant title
- The title now hangs as a purple velvet pennant from a gold rod — gold lettering, a scatter of tiny stars, a zigzag hem with gold tassels — matching the curtains.

## 2026-11-22 — Celestial title plaque

## 2026-11-22 — Paper-cut moon
- The moon is now a gold paper-cut crescent hanging on a string from behind the curtain, like a stage prop. Tap it for the note for Mom.

## 2026-11-22 — Purple celestial curtains
- The stage curtains are now deep purple velvet scattered with little gold stars and crescent moons — on the closed opening curtain and on the tied-back curtains and drapes around the tree. The back drapes along the top are midnight blue.

## 2026-11-22 — Livelier hanging stars on phones
- On phones the hanging stars now drop to clearly different lengths, from just under the curtain to just above the tree, and a few are noticeably bigger. The tree sits a little lower to make room.

## 2026-11-22 — Toolbar steps aside during the story
- While the story plays, the bottom buttons are hidden and the story card moves down to the stage edge, giving the tree more room. Skip or "Explore the tree" brings them back.

## 2026-11-22 — Story flicker found and fixed
- The flash during the story was the hidden person-info panel briefly sliding across the screen each time a new chapter picked a person. It now stays hidden.

## 2026-11-22 — Calmer tree on phones
- On phones, story chapters now change their highlighting while the tree is faded out, and the pulsing glows on faces and the twinkling memory stars inside the tree hold still, so the phone isn't constantly redrawing the whole tree.
- The theater sign is drawn on its own layer so it no longer flickers when things change behind it.

## 2026-11-22 — Story steps on phones, smaller sign
- On phones, moving between story chapters now fades the tree out and back in at its new spot instead of sliding, which stops the flickering "double picture".
- The theater sign is smaller on phones, leaving more room for the tree.
- Ending the story on a phone returns to the usual close-up of the family instead of the whole tree.

## 2026-11-22 — Smoother story on phones
- Moving between story chapters now glides the tree as one picture instead of redrawing it frame by frame, so the leaves and faces move together without stuttering or jumping. The highlighting of each chapter's people now happens once the move has finished.

## 2026-11-22 — Family photos on the opening card
- The slowly scrolling family photos are back on the opening screen, now inside the card only, softly faded so the words stay easy to read. The curtains around it stay clean.

## 2026-11-22 — More hanging stars
- About twice as many gold stars now hang from the curtain, in different sizes and at different drops, filling the open sky beside the tree (and the gap under the sign on phones) without covering any faces.

## 2026-11-22 — Sign at the top, buttons at the bottom
- The marquee sign and the newest-memory line now sit at the very top of the stage, hanging over the curtain.
- The buttons moved to a row along the stage edge at the bottom; the Program menu opens upward.
- The whole family now fits between the sign and the buttons when the tree opens or you press Fit.

## 2026-11-22 — Smoother curtain opening
- The curtains no longer jump or bounce before they open. They now wait for the screen to finish going full screen, and the curtain frame no longer changes shape as the phone's bars slide away.

## 2026-11-22 — Everything on stage
- The tree now stays full screen; there's no way to accidentally leave the stage.
- A "Program" menu at the top opens the Chart, Map, Timeline and Sources, and a "Can you help?" button opens the family questions. Each opens as a page over the stage; "Back to the tree" (or Escape) closes it.

## 2026-11-22 — Opening curtain
- The opening screen is now a closed red stage curtain, with the dedication, title, faces and both buttons on a gold-edged card in front.
- The tree is already standing behind the curtain. Choosing either button pulls the curtains open to reveal it — no more growing animation, so it opens smoothly on phones.

## 2026-11-22 — The stage
- The tree now stands on a little theater stage: red curtains with gold fringe, a painted backdrop with cut-out clouds and gold stars on strings, footlights along the bottom, gold-rimmed sun and moon, and engraved shading on the trunk and planet.
- The family name is a lit marquee sign fixed at the top, with the newest memories just under it.
- The tree always opens at night. The sky button still switches to sunset during a visit.
- Simpler controls: the search box, zoom buttons and larger-text button are gone (pinch or scroll to zoom).

## 2026-11-22 — Worn paper name scrolls
- Name scrolls and the family banner now look like old, worn paper: slightly rough, torn edges, aged colouring that darkens toward the edges, a faint stain here and there, and shaded ribbon ends that fold behind.
- Fixed the dotted outline that showed around the scrolls when zoomed in.

## 2026-11-22 — Constellations off, banner raised
- The G and H constellations beside the banner are hidden for now.
- The family banner and the newest-memory line sit higher in the sky, with clear space between them and the top of the tree.

- Just under it, a small line shows the newest memories as they're shared ("Pam shared a memory about Ed · 2 hours ago"), changing every few seconds. Tap it to read the memory. Before anyone has posted, it invites people to tap a face and share one.

## 2026-11-22 — Sun behind the trunk

## 2026-11-22 — Names centered under every photo

- More room along the planet for the brothers, their wives and the grandchildren, so their names no longer crowd each other.

- The leaves no longer flash while the tree grows on Android phones. The canopy is now painted in one smooth sweep as the tree grows.

- The leaves no longer blink or shift sideways once the tree has finished growing.
- Name scrolls that were nudged apart no longer slide into place after they appear.

## 2026-11-21 — Tree grows in full
- When the tree first appears, the full painted canopy now grows out together with the branches, instead of a bare tree appearing first and the leaves filling in afterwards.

## 2026-11-21 — New painted tree
- The tree is now a full, hand-painted tree with a thick canopy of leaves and a sturdy trunk planted on the planet. The sky, moon, planet and hometown roots are unchanged.
- Names sit on small paper scrolls; neighbouring names no longer run into each other.
- Memory stars are bigger and brighter, and each one now twinkles in the leaves right beside the person the memory is about. Tap a star to read it.
- The printable poster uses the new tree too.

## 2026-11-21 — "For Mom"
- The dedication now reads "For Mom" everywhere: the welcome screen, the intro line, the poster, the last story chapter, and the note hidden in the moon and sun.

## 2026-11-21 — New background music
- New background song. It starts right away (no silent gap), plays its opening once, then loops smoothly without the ending fade-out.

## 2026-11-21 — Voice memories and safer posting
- Record a voice memory (up to 2 minutes) when sharing a story; typing is optional when there's a recording. Music pauses while recording and fades down during playback.
- Voice memories play from the person's card and from memory stars on the tree.
- Photo uploads are back — the option to add a photo was hidden after the move to GitHub.
- After too many wrong passcodes, adding is paused for 15 minutes.
- Posts can only be removed from the phone or computer that added them; the code that identifies a device is no longer visible in the shared data.
- Behind the scenes: the page is now built from separate source files.

## 2026-11-21 — Moved to GitHub Pages
- The tree now opens for anyone with the link — no account or sign-in.
- Shared memories, photos and answers are saved to Mat's Google Sheet. Adding something asks for the family passcode once per device.
- Search engines are asked not to list the site.
- Poster QR code now points to artificialfear.github.io/family-tree.

## 2026-11-21 — Smoother on phones
- Dragging and pinching now slide a ready-made picture of the tree instead of redrawing it every frame, then sharpen it when you let go.
- Moving updates are bundled into one per screen refresh; the page no longer re-measures itself while you drag.
- Lighter effects: fewer stars on phones, softer clouds without blur, no glowing shadows on hometown markers, no swaying foliage on phones, smaller background image on phones. Animations pause while dragging.
- Ending the story no longer pops open Renee's details over the tree.

## 2026-11-21 — Android fixes
- Lifting one finger after a pinch no longer makes the tree jump to a different spot.
- The tree's picture layers stay ready all the time, so starting or ending a drag no longer flashes.
- Long, fast drags refresh the picture before its pre-drawn edges run out, instead of showing blank patches.
- The phone's address bar appearing/disappearing mid-drag no longer snaps the view.
